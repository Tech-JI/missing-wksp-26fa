\section{TechGC Introduction}

\begin{frame}{Who are we?}
\begin{itemize}
    \item TechGC's \textbf{Technical Department} is a hands-on platform to explore, learn, and build.
    \item Develop practical skills in engineering and information technology through real projects.
    \item \textbf{New to technology? No worries!} Learn the fundamentals with teammates who are ready to grow together.
    \item Our division of work is flexible: choose the groups that match your interests.
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Workshop Outreach}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Organize workshops such as Linux Install Party, Git, and Bash.
    \item Help students learn useful technical skills and meet like-minded teammates.
\end{itemize}

\begin{frame}{Technical Maintenance}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Keep existing projects, tools, and services running smoothly and up to date.
    \item Maintain and operate the department's website.
    \item Gain hands-on experience and develop problem-solving skills by supporting real services.
\end{itemize}

\begin{frame}{Project Development}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Turn new ideas into real products through design and development.

\begin{frame}{Publicity}

    \item Develop communication, design, teamwork, and content creation skills.

\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Find your place in TechGC}
